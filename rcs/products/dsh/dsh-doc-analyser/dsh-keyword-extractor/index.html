Welcome to DSH Keyword Extractor

Document Smart Highlights Keyword Extractor

The keyword extraction is executed by setting scores to each term in the document's text. This uses a TF/IDF approach through several different implementations of TF and IDF, as shown in this paper.

Those terms having the best scores obtained by a meta-algorithmic combination of all TF/IDF combinations will then be returned as the best ranked keywords.
